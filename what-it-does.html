LARRIE listens to a live signal, recognises the patterns that recur within it, and infers what is coming next. From a single audio stream it produces timestamped event detections, recurring-pattern and structure maps, and real-time predictions that can drive whatever sits downstream — from light to diagnosis.

In development since 2020, LARRIE began as a college thesis project and has grown into a general engine for recurrence in sound. It has been used to predict heart attacks — and save lives — and the same inference core can be applied to Formula 1 engine diagnostics.

Along the way it has performed where it started: driving live visuals in small venues and living rooms internationally.
